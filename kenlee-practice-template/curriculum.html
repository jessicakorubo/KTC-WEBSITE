NABTEB

National Business & Technical Examinations Board

NABTEB is Nigeria's premier examining body for technical and vocational education. Our NABTEB programme equips students with industry-standard skills and nationally recognised certificates in trade and technical disciplines — ideal for students seeking direct entry into skilled employment or entrepreneurship.

JUPEB

Joint Universities Preliminary Examinations Board

JUPEB is a one-year advanced-level programme that grants successful students direct 200-level admission into any affiliated Nigerian university — bypassing the UTME entirely. Our intensive classes are led by experienced university-level tutors committed to getting every student into their dream institution.

Senior Secondary

WAEC

West African Examinations Council — WASSCE

Our WAEC (WASSCE) programme is a rigorous three-year Senior Secondary curriculum that prepares students to obtain the West African Senior School Certificate — the gold standard for secondary-level qualifications recognised across West Africa and beyond. We offer Science, Commercial, and Arts tracks.
